Heimatplatz is your real estate platform for Upper Austria. Find houses, building plots and foreclosure auctions – clear, fast and without detours.

DISCOVER PROPERTIES
Browse current listings from all over Upper Austria. Filter by property type, time period and location – single municipalities or whole districts. Sort by price, area or recency – and find what suits you. Heimatplatz remembers your filters automatically.

MAP VIEW
Take a look at your search as a map: district stamps show where how many properties are located – tap one to zoom straight into that district. Zoom in and the stamps turn into price signs right at the location. Tap one to open the mini note with photo and key facts – from there you go straight to the full listing. Your filters apply on the map just the same. And every listing has a map of its own under “Location & surroundings”.

FORECLOSURE AUCTIONS
If you like, Heimatplatz shows you current auctions from the official Austrian edict database – simply enable them in the filter. With appraised value, minimum bid, auction date, encumbrances and court information, you have all the details at a glance. Right in the list you can see how many days are left until the auction.

PRIVATE OR AGENT
Decide for yourself: filter specifically for private listings or offers from agents and property managers. Heimatplatz transparently shows you who is behind every listing.

FAVORITES & NOTIFICATIONS
Save interesting properties as favorites. Enable push notifications for your preferred locations and get informed immediately when new properties go online.

LIST YOUR OWN PROPERTY – ALL ON ONE PAGE
Want to sell? You design your listing right in the view it will later appear in: photos, key facts, location and price, description – all on one page. Your draft is saved automatically – pause any time and continue later. For the description, AI can help if you like: type a few keywords or dictate them – you review the finished text, adjust it and publish. Your photos are kept in full original quality. You decide for yourself how precisely your property appears on the map: pin-point exact, only roughly within a circle, or not at all – with a live preview. If you like, name a dedicated contact person with their own contact details. Manage your ads in your personal area.

DIRECT LINE TO OUR TEAM
Questions, ideas or a problem? Reach out to our team right from the app – as text, with photos or as a voice message. You get the reply right in the app and are notified as soon as it arrives.

PRIVACY
Heimatplatz is built on data minimization. Browse anonymously – no account needed. Your data belongs to you.
