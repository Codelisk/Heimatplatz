Heimatplatz is your real estate platform for Upper Austria. Find houses, building plots and foreclosure auctions – clear, fast and without detours.

DISCOVER PROPERTIES
Browse current listings from all over Upper Austria. Filter by property type, location and time period. Sort by price, area or recency – and find exactly what suits you.

FORECLOSURE AUCTIONS
If you like, Heimatplatz shows you current auctions from the official Austrian edict database – simply enable them in the filter. With appraised value, minimum bid, auction date and court information, you have all the details at a glance.

PRIVATE OR AGENT
Decide for yourself: filter specifically for private listings or offers from agents and property managers. Heimatplatz transparently shows you who is behind every listing.

FAVORITES & NOTIFICATIONS
Save interesting properties as favorites. Enable push notifications for your preferred locations and get informed immediately when new properties go online.

LIST YOUR OWN PROPERTY – ALL ON ONE PAGE
Want to sell? You design your listing right in the view it will later appear in: photos, key facts, location and price, description – all on one page. Your draft is saved automatically – pause any time and continue later. For the description, AI can help if you like: type a few keywords or simply dictate them – you review the finished text, adjust it and publish. Your photos are kept in full original quality. If you like, you can name a dedicated contact person with their own contact details for your listing. Manage your ads conveniently in your personal area.

DIRECT LINE TO OUR TEAM
Questions, ideas or a problem? Reach out to our team right from the app – as text, with photos or simply as a voice message. You get the reply right in the app and are notified as soon as it arrives.

PRIVACY
Heimatplatz is built on data minimization. You can browse anonymously without creating an account. Your data belongs to you.
