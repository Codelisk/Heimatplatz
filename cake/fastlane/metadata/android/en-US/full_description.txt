Heimatplatz is your real estate platform for Upper Austria. Find houses, building plots and foreclosure auctions – clear, fast and without detours.

DISCOVER PROPERTIES
Browse current listings from all over Upper Austria. Filter by property type, location and time period. Sort by price, area or recency – and find exactly what suits you.

FORECLOSURE AUCTIONS
Heimatplatz shows you current auctions from the official Austrian edict database. With appraised value, minimum bid, auction date and court information, you have all the details at a glance.

PRIVATE OR AGENT
Decide for yourself: filter specifically for private listings or offers from agents and property managers. Heimatplatz transparently shows you who is behind every listing.

FAVORITES & NOTIFICATIONS
Save interesting properties as favorites. Enable push notifications for your preferred locations and get informed immediately when new properties go online.

LIST YOUR OWN PROPERTY – STEP BY STEP
Want to sell? The wizard guides you to a finished listing in five steps: photos, key facts, location and price, description, preview. Your draft is saved automatically – pause any time and continue later. For the description, AI can help if you like: type a few keywords or simply dictate them – you review the finished text, adjust it and publish. Manage your ads conveniently in your personal area.

PRIVACY
Heimatplatz is built on data minimization. You can browse anonymously without creating an account. Your data belongs to you.
